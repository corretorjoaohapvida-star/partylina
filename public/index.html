Frequently asked questions

Is this a physical product?

No. This is a digital download. No assembled products, paper, printer or tools are included.

Is the artwork fully editable?

No. The designs are predesigned, so layouts, backgrounds and decorative elements cannot all be changed. Selected files allow a name to be added. Canva editing links are not included.

Can I add my customer’s name?

Selected files allow you to add a name. This does not make the complete design fully editable.

Do I need a cutting machine?

You can print suitable PDF designs and cut them by hand. STUDIO files use a separate Silhouette Studio workflow. Cricut compatibility has not been verified for this collection, and SVG files are not included.

Which file formats and paper size do I receive?

The collection includes PDF, PNG and STUDIO files; availability varies by design. The artwork uses A4. Check your printer’s settings and test the chosen design before making a full order.

Is all of the artwork in English?

No. This is the original Lina Criativa artwork collection, and some designs and previews contain Portuguese text. The purchase does not include a fully translated artwork library.

Can I sell the pieces I make?

Lina Criativa permits you to sell physical items you make using the files. You may not resell, share or redistribute the digital files. This permission covers Lina Criativa’s rights; it does not grant additional rights to third-party characters or brands.

When will I receive access?

Digital access is provided after Hotmart approves your payment. Your purchase confirmation includes the access instructions.

What’s the difference between Basic and Complete?

Basic is $7.90 USD and includes 100 selected designs, without bonuses. Complete is $14.90 USD and includes 1,000+ designs plus the mini coloring book bundle with 30+ themes, promotion photos, materials list and WhatsApp messages.

Is there a guarantee or a subscription?

Both plans are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. Both include a 7-day money-back guarantee. Request your refund through Hotmart within 7 days of purchase.
