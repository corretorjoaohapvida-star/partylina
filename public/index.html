BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few things
you may be wondering.

What’s included, how to use the files, and how your purchase works.

What do I receive?

A digital collection of party designs. Basic includes 100 selected designs. Complete includes 1,000+ designs and the four bonuses shown above. Paper, tools and assembled pieces are not included.

Can I print at home without a cutting machine?

Use suitable PDF designs for printing and cutting by hand. The artwork uses A4 paper size. STUDIO files are for a separate Silhouette Studio workflow; availability varies by design.

Can I edit the designs or add a name?

The artwork is predesigned. Selected files allow a name to be added, but the complete layouts and decorative elements are not fully editable. Canva links and SVG files are not included; Cricut compatibility has not been verified.

Is all the artwork in English?

This is the original Lina Criativa collection. Some artwork and previews contain Portuguese text. The purchase includes the original files, rather than a fully translated artwork library.

Can I sell the physical pieces I make?

Lina Criativa permits physical-item sales using the files. Digital files may not be resold, shared or redistributed. This permission covers Lina Criativa’s rights and does not grant additional rights to third-party characters or brands.

How do I get my files?

Hotmart provides access instructions after your payment is approved. Look for the purchase confirmation using the email you enter at checkout.

Which plan includes the bonuses?

Complete, at $14.90 USD, includes the full 1,000+ collection, the party planner and production checklist, promotion photos, materials list and WhatsApp messages. Basic, at $7.90 USD, includes 100 selected designs without the bonuses.

Is this a subscription, and is there a guarantee?

Both plans are one-time purchases. Both include a 7-day money-back guarantee. You can request a refund through Hotmart within 7 days of purchase.
